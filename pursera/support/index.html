Pursera

Support

Pursera is a subscription and bill tracker made in Sweden.

Quick answers

  • I don't get notifications. Check iPhone Settings → Pursera → Notifications, and that no Focus mode is blocking them.
  • How do I change the language? Settings → Language. The change applies the next time you open the app.
  • How do I start the free trial? Settings → Try Pro Free. You can cancel any time in your Apple ID subscriptions.
  • How do I move my data to a new phone? Sign in to the same iCloud account; your data syncs automatically.
  • How do I export my data? Pro: Settings → Export CSV.
  • How do I delete an item? Open it and use Delete, or swipe it in a list.
