Pursera

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Pursera is a subscription, bill and asset tracker developed in Sweden. It is designed so that your data stays with you. (Earlier versions of the app were listed under the working name AssetXpense; this policy covers them too.)

What Pursera stores

Everything you enter: subscriptions, bills, assets, amounts, dates, notes, categories and photos you attach. This data lives on your device.

No tracking, no servers

  • Pursera has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no account system of its own.
  • The developer does not receive, see or store your data.
  • Pursera does not track you across apps or websites.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, your data is kept in your private iCloud (CloudKit) database so it appears on your other devices. Only you can access it. The developer cannot. iCloud is managed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to remind you before a payment or due date. These are scheduled on your device.
  • Photos / Camera – only if you attach a picture to an item. Only the pictures you choose are read.
  • Face ID – optional app lock for Pro users. Authentication is handled by iOS; Pursera never receives your biometric data.

Subscription

Pursera Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers) billed by Apple. Payment and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details.

Exports

CSV files you export are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.

Deleting your data

Delete an item inside the app, or delete the app to remove local data. To remove iCloud data, use Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

Pursera is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with an updated date.
