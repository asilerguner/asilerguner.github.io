Features

Know what you pay, before you pay it

Add your subscriptions and bills once, and Pursera keeps watch over every renewal and due date.

Reminders before every payment

Get a heads-up before a subscription renews or a bill is due, so there are no surprise charges.

All subscriptions in one list

Streaming, apps, gym, insurance, rent. Add them in seconds with quick-start templates.

See what you really spend

Monthly and yearly totals across all your recurring payments, in multiple currencies.

Calendar view

Privacy

Your finances stay yours

Pursera has no server of its own. Your data lives on your device and, if you like, in your own iCloud, so it is there on all your devices without ever passing through anyone else.
