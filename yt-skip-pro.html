YT Skip Pro

Free YouTube ad skipper for Chrome

A small browser extension that clicks Skip on YouTube video ads as soon as the button appears, and hides ad slots in your feed and on watch pages. It is not a full ad blocker: ads without a Skip button still play.

Free for everyone. No account, no tracking, no data leaves your browser.

Install in five steps

The extension is not in the Chrome Web Store, so you add it yourself. It takes about a minute and works the same in Chrome, Edge and Brave.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download yt-skip-pro-1.1.2.zip and unzip it. Put the yt-skip-pro folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents. The browser reads the extension from this folder, so do not delete or move it afterwards.

  2. Open the extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter. In Edge use edge://extensions, in Brave brave://extensions.

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    Switch on Developer mode, the toggle at the top right of the extensions page. This is what allows you to add an extension from a folder.

  4. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked, choose the unzipped yt-skip-pro folder (the one that contains manifest.json) and confirm. YT Skip Pro now appears in your list of extensions.

  5. Pin it and reload YouTube

    Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin YT Skip Pro so you can open its settings. Then reload any open YouTube tabs. From now on it works by itself.

Good to know

  • Chrome may show a notice about developer-mode extensions when you start the browser. You can dismiss it.
  • To update: download the new zip, replace the old folder, then click the reload button on the extension's card at chrome://extensions.
  • To remove: click Remove on the extension's card at chrome://extensions.

What it does

Auto-skip, ad hiding and banners each have a switch in the popup.

Auto-skip ads

When YouTube shows a skippable ad, the extension clicks the Skip button the moment it appears.

Hide feed ads

Removes sponsored slots and promoted cards from the home page and search results.

Hide watch-page ads

Removes the ad panel next to the video you are watching.

Hide banners and overlays

Closes the small ad overlays on the video and hides banner areas at the top of the page.

Settings that follow you

Your choices are stored by your browser and sync across the browsers you are signed in to.

Warning notice

If YouTube shows a warning about ad blockers, the popup tells you, so you know why and can switch the extension off.

Private and free

Frequently asked questions

Does it skip every ad?

No. It clicks Skip on ads that offer a Skip button and hides ad slots on the page. Ads that cannot be skipped still play. The extension never mutes or fast-forwards them, because YouTube detects that easily.

Will it keep working?

YouTube changes its page often and may break any extension of this kind without notice. If it stops working, download the latest version from this page.

YouTube shows a warning about ad blockers. What now?

Switch the extension off in the popup and reload the page. The popup shows a notice when YouTube has shown such a warning.

Why is it not in the Chrome Web Store?

It is shared here directly and for free. Adding it from a folder takes one minute, and the steps above show how.

Does it work on a phone?

No. It is a desktop extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave.

Is this affiliated with YouTube or Google?

No. YT Skip Pro is an independent personal project. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC. Many creators earn from ads, so consider supporting the channels you enjoy in other ways.
