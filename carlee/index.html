Features

Everything about your car, in one place

Log it in seconds, see what your car really costs, and never miss an inspection or insurance renewal again.

Fuel & consumption

Log every fill-up and follow your consumption over time, with odometer scanning to save typing.

Every cost, tracked

Service, insurance, tax, loans, parking and tolls, plus depreciation, so you see the true cost of ownership.

Smart reminders Premium

Inspection, service, insurance and tax dates, with a heads-up one month ahead, one week ahead, or on the day.

Trips on the map Premium

Record trips manually or automatically and review each route on a map.

Documents & photos

Attach receipts, registration papers and photos to a car or to any single entry.

Reports & backups Premium

PDF reports per car, CSV exports for Excel, and full backups whenever you want them.

Privacy

Your car data stays yours

Carlee has no server of its own. Your data lives on your devices and, if you like, in your own iCloud, so it is there on iPhone, iPad and Mac without ever passing through anyone else.
