Carlee – My Car

Fuel, costs & car reminders. Made in Sweden.

Support Privacy Policy

Everything about your car, in one place

Carlee keeps fuel and expenses, service and inspection reminders, trips, valuations, notes, photos and documents together - privately, on your own devices, and in sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. No ads, no tracking, no account.

  • Fuel, costs and consumption
  • Service, inspection, insurance & tax reminders
  • Trip meter
  • PDF reports
  • Odometer scanning
  • Private iCloud sync

Pricing

Free to download with one car. Carlee Premium unlocks unlimited cars, PDF reports for every car and odometer scanning: $9.99 per year (or the equivalent in your local currency) with a 7-day free trial. The subscription renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time in your Apple ID settings.
