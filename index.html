How I bring AI into everyday work

Five steps I follow each time, and I go around again.

  1. Market analysis

    Start with what AI can really do today, what vendors offer and what others are doing.

  2. Find the repeat

    Look for the checks and decisions people make over and over.

  3. Prototype on real data

    Build a working version fast and test it on real data.

  4. Make it easy to use

    Put it inside the tools people already use, so it needs no special training.

  5. Train, measure, iterate

    Train people, check who really uses it, and keep improving it with their feedback.
