A calm way to run your appointments

Ontime is an appointment manager for independent practices and small businesses: salons, clinics, therapists, trainers, tutors and anyone who works by appointment. Your calendar, clients, invoices and online bookings live in one native app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and customers book from a web page that needs no app.

Set up your company profile

Setup takes about ten minutes. You can change everything later.

  1. Create your businessOpen the app and choose Sign in to keep your data in the cloud and share it with a team, or Use on this device only. Enter your name, the business name, type, currency and time zone.
  2. Add your servicesMore → Services. Give each service a name, length and price, and an optional buffer after each appointment.
  3. Add your team and working hoursMore → Team. Set each person's weekly hours. Nothing can be booked outside them.
  4. Fill in your invoice detailsMore → Invoice template. Add business details, how to pay and your terms once; they appear on every invoice.
  5. Turn on online bookingMore → Online booking. Choose which services and people appear, copy your link or embed code, and press Open booking page to see what customers see.
  6. Set your booking rulesMinimum notice, how far ahead, time steps, a gap after each booking and days off. Enter your email so customers can reply to confirmations.
  7. Add your clientsClients tab → +. Phone and email are needed to send messages.

How online booking works

Customers pick a service, a person and a free time on your booking page and enter their name, phone and email. You choose how requests are handled: wait for your answer, or let free times book at once, appear in your calendar and be confirmed by email. The customer receives the email in their own language, and you get a notification on your phone.

Customers become clients when a booking is confirmed. Times shown on the page are refreshed whenever you open the app or change rules; if a time is taken in the meantime, you are told before anything is confirmed.

User guide

Daily work

  • Today lists the day's appointments. Tap one to mark it arrived, finished or no-show, or to cancel it.
  • Calendar has day and week views. Tap an empty time to book, or tap an appointment to change it.
  • Clients holds details, history, notes and bills for each customer.
  • Billing lists invoices and records cash, card-terminal or transfer payments.

Book the next visit

Open a finished appointment and choose Book next. Pick how many weeks ahead; Ontime suggests the nearest free time, shows your free times for the chosen day and the whole day with your other appointments, and books it when you confirm. Tap a free spot on the day view to choose that time.

Colour groups for clients

In a client's details, set a Group such as an insurance company, and choose a colour. Every client in the same group shares it, and appointments show in that colour on the calendar with a legend.

Messages and reminders

Ontime prepares the message and opens Messages or Mail; you press send. Edit the reminder, confirmed, declined and cancelled texts under More → Message templates. Words in double braces are replaced with the customer's name, date, time and person.

Team and sync

Signed in, your business is stored in the cloud and teammates join with an invite code. More → Account & sync shows the state and offers Sync now.

Deleting things

  • A service with bookings cannot be deleted; switch it off instead. Existing appointments keep their time and price.
  • A person can be deactivated; their appointments stay and they are no longer offered for booking.
  • A client can be deleted with all their records, which also frees their times.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to try
Booking page shows no timesCheck that the service and person are switched on for online booking, the person has working hours, and your notice period and days off are not blocking everything.
A request cannot be confirmedThe time was taken, is now a day off, or the service or person was switched off. Decline and offer another time.
No alert for a requestCheck notification permission and Background App Refresh in iOS Settings, and open the app to see the badge.
Sync says waitingCheck the internet connection, then use Sync now.

Pricing

Ontime is free for a solo business. Premium is $5 per month to start with, with a 30-day free trial, and is needed only if you:

  • turn on automatic confirmation emails,
  • have more than one employee, or
  • have more than 20 appointments in a month.
