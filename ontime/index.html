A calm way to run your appointments

Ontime is an appointment manager for independent practices and small businesses: salons, clinics, therapists, trainers, tutors and anyone who works by appointment. Keep your calendar, clients and invoices in one app, and let customers book themselves from a web page that needs no app.

  • Day and week calendar, no double bookings
  • Services, working hours and team members
  • Online booking page for any browser
  • Automatic confirmation emails
  • Reminder emails the day before
  • Clients and invoices
  • Booking page in 23 languages

How online booking works

Customers pick a service and a free time on your booking page. You choose whether each request waits for your answer, or whether free times are booked at once, added to your calendar and confirmed by email without you lifting a finger. The customer gets the email in their own language, and you get a notification on your phone.

Pricing

A 30-day free trial, then a subscription: $10 per month or $99 per year. After the trial you can still view and export everything; creating new appointments and accepting online bookings needs a subscription. Prices are shown in the App Store at launch.
